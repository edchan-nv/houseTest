A QUICK GUIDE

How to use this tour

Download the editable model ↗
01

Choose your view

Select Room tour, then tap a room thumbnail below the image. Use Main level or Lower level to see the floor layout, or Exterior to see the house.

02

Remove the staging

Choose Empty to hide all staging, or Furnished to restore it. In room views, toggle seating, tables, beds, and decor separately. Reset restores all groups. Walls and built-ins stay in place.

03

Turn and compare

In exterior and floor views, swipe or drag left and right, use the arrow buttons, or press your keyboard’s left/right arrows. Each step turns 15°. Room viewpoints are fixed. Tap ⛶ to expand where supported.

04

Take the model with you

Download and unzip the model. Open house.usda in a USD-compatible app or house.blend in Blender. The furnishing variant hides all staging; individual items are listed in furniture-prims.json.

These are pre-rendered views of an approximate, photo-guided model. You can switch views and staging here; moving individual furniture items or navigating freely requires the editable model. Copy your browser’s URL to share the current view.
